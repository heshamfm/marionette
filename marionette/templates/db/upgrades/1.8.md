# Marionette 1.8: rules to add to the owner's manual

The upgrade program changes the programs and the data. It never writes the owner's manual: the manual is the owner's.
The session that applies the upgrade shows the owner these rules, in the owner's language, and adds each one the owner
approves to MANUAL.md (the section named), raising its version and logging the decision. Scheduled runs name them in
their message until the owner decides.

## Section 3, item rules
Late: a for-information item is never late. It is told once and closed (marked seen); one that asks something of the
owner is a decision or a reply, never "for information".
Checks due: when the live check finds nothing new on an item waiting on others, its next check moves a few working days
ahead, not to the next day, and the quote says what was checked for that item.

## Section 4, the periodic run
First, after the quick steps: apply what the owner submitted on the panel (tools/db_usage.md, "The panel").
Delegated items (brief DELEGATED TO MARIONETTE): do, prepare or follow the owner's instructions as db_usage.md says.
If run-start names missed or late scheduled runs, say so first in the message and record an issue.
At the end: refresh the panel. When the owner chose it, the phone notification is one headline line and the panel link.

## Section 6, the auditor
A fix is not finished until the measure that stops the same fault coming back is in place: a guard in the program, a
step in the run's order, or a rule in its home file. When the measure needs the owner's approval, it is proposed and
asked. Every defect is logged beside its measure.
